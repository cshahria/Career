💡 Sprint Guide: How Percentages, Pulse & Reports Work ▼

1. Progress Tracking: Type your percentage completion directly into the large digit box or use arrow keys (20% increments).

2. Daily Pulse: Click 🟢 On Track, 🟡 Review, or 🔴 Help to update status on the left.

3. Primary Goal: Type your multi-word primary sprint goal directly into your row.

4. Progress Report Submission: Click 📝 Report on your row to log detailed weekly goals directly to the Master Spreadsheet.
