📖 System Documentation & Best Practices

1. Weekly Cycle & Reset (Monday – Sunday)

The weekly tracking period runs strictly from Monday (00:00 PDT) through Sunday (23:59 PDT). Every Monday morning, the weekly table automatically shifts focus to the new 40-hour goal.

Data Isolation Guarantee: Historical entries from prior weeks remain safely archived in your Google Sheet and under the Daily History table. They will never affect or cause adverse calculations on subsequent weekly resets.

2. Timesheet Submissions & Weekend Work

Extract your weekly summary on Friday evenings via 📷 Save PNG or 📥 Export CSV for timesheet submission. If you happen to work over the weekend, you can extract additional ad-hoc reports at any time before Sunday midnight.

3. Correcting Forgotten Clock-Outs or Missed Breaks

If you forget to clock out or miss logging a break, do not fix it with repeated button clicks:

  • Open the linked Google Sheet directly from the top menu button.
  • Locate the corresponding row under the Timestamp column.
  • Manually update the time string or edit the Work Performed / Notes cell to reflect correct start/end times.
  • Return to this dashboard and click ↻ Refresh to recalculate all duration metrics.

4. Initial Onboarding Verification

During your first few days, export your CSV or PNG daily after clocking out. Compare the hours recorded in the app against your Google Sheet to verify network logging accuracy.
